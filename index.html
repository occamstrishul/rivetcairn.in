Don't replace a factory. Modernize it.

Most of what's already on your floor still works. Rivetcairn maps how it runs — every machine, bottleneck, manual step, and quality checkpoint — and designs automation that fits around it instead of replacing it outright.

Less capital at risk, a shorter path to running, and a factory that keeps the equipment you already know.

What we do

Factory assessment

We walk the floor, time the cycles, and map bottlenecks and manual steps into a single Automation Report.

Systems design & integration

Conveyors, robotic arms, sensors, machine vision, PLCs and SCADA, specified around the machinery you already own.

Implementation

We source, build, program, test and install the system on your floor, and stay until it runs the way it was designed to.
